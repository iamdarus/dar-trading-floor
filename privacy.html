Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

This explains what DAR Trading Floor collects, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as the app can work with, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

Account: your email address, or the (possibly relayed) address Apple gives us when you use Sign in with Apple. That is the only identifier we require.

Learning data: your display name, chosen difficulty, XP, rank, streak, completed lessons, lesson scores, achievements and review schedule. This is what syncs between your devices.

Content you write: community posts, trade journals, replies and live-room messages, plus the questions you ask the AI Mentor and the Lab coach.

Purchase data: your subscription status, supplied by RevenueCat and Apple. We never see your card details.

Technical data: notification token if you enable reminders, and standard server logs.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or browsing history, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Who else processes it

Supabase — database, authentication and hosting for everything above. Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure.

Anthropic — receives the text of the questions you ask the AI Mentor and the Lab coach, the community posts that are machine-translated, and lesson text for translation, in order to generate a response. Ask the Mentor nothing you would not want processed by a third party. Anthropic does not train models on this data under its commercial terms.

RevenueCat and Apple — subscription purchase, renewal and entitlement status.

Expo — app delivery and over-the-air updates.

These are processors acting on our instructions. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Why we are allowed to (legal basis)

Performance of our contract with you: running your account, syncing progress, delivering the membership you bought.

Legitimate interests: keeping the community safe, preventing abuse and fraud, and fixing the app.

Consent: push notifications, which you can turn off at any time in Settings or in iOS.

How long we keep it

Account and learning data: until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Posts: deleted with your account. Copies quoted by other members in their own replies may remain.

Cached machine translations of your posts are deleted with the post.

Server logs: a short rolling window for operational and security purposes.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data yourself, at any time, from Account → Delete account. No email, no waiting period.

Depending on where you live (including the UK, EEA, Switzerland, Brazil and California) you also have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or object to processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, or to ask what we hold about you, write to art.dilbaryan@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is for adults. It is not directed at children, we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and accounts found to belong to minors are deleted.

International transfers and security

Our processors operate internationally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Access is protected by database row-level security and transport encryption. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the relevant authority as required by law.

Changes and contact

We will surface material changes in the app. Questions or requests: art.dilbaryan@gmail.com.
